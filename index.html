08 Process

Clarity before scale.

A production becomes reliable when creative, financial and operational decisions live in the same system.

  1. 01

    Read

    Brief, references, goals, audience, usage and constraints.

  2. 02

    Test

    Feasibility, scope, locations, timing, rights and budget corridor.

  3. 03

    Build

    Treatment, crew, schedule, logistics, technical plan and estimate.

  4. 04

    Produce

    One decision flow from preparation through set operations.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Post production, approvals, masters, adaptations and archive.
