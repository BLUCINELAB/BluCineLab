BluCineLab is a Bologna-based creative and production studio. For projects coming into Italy, we can scope feasibility, local collaborators, locations, logistics and the production structure needed around an existing or developing brief.

We are not a volume fixer or a generic service directory. The best fit is work where production decisions and visual judgement need to stay connected.

FAQ

Before the first call.

Where are you based?

Bologna, Emilia-Romagna. Projects elsewhere in Italy are evaluated case by case around timing, scope and the local support required.

Can you join an existing international crew?

Yes. Tell us which roles are already attached and what needs to be solved locally so responsibilities stay clear from the start.

Do you manage permissions?

Permission and access requirements depend on the location and production. We assess them during feasibility and coordinate the appropriate local route rather than promising a generic permit package.

Do you provide only production service?

Sometimes the need is purely local support. The strongest fit, however, is when production and visual decision-making benefit from staying connected.

What should we send first?

Brief or reference, city or region, approximate dates, expected outputs, attached crew and any known constraints. Budget can still be open.
