The commission

A precise visual vocabulary for cocktails, food and rooms with history.

This is an earlier commission from Anton’s still-life and hospitality portfolio, retained because it shows an ability BluCineLab still brings to image-led projects: controlled surfaces, atmosphere and a strong sense of place.

I Conoscenti occupies Palazzo Conoscenti in central Bologna. The photographs move between intimate still life, the action of service and the building’s layered interiors.

Credit note: selected commission by Anton Likht. It is not presented as a current BluCineLab client relationship.
