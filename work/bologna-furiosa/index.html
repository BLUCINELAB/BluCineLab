The work

Cultural production where participation, representation and a real public space meet.

Bologna Furiosa brought ballroom culture into a collective event format. Anton Likht and Sonia Giudici were part of the team behind its first edition.

The work remains in the selection because it extends the studio’s practice beyond a conventional shoot: production is also the design of conditions in which bodies, community and an audience can take space.
