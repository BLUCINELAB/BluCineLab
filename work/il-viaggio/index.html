The work

A psychological passage through place, recollection and an uncertain return.

Il viaggio is presented as selected work by BluCineLab’s two core members, not retrospectively relabelled as a studio production.

Sonia Giudici directed, edited and coordinated production. Anton Likht shaped the cinematography. The resulting film uses a restrained landscape and a red figure as recurring visual anchors.
