Why it remains

Useful thinking can be shown without pretending that development is delivery.

The Cube explores how a moving image can become a spatial encounter: a modular volume, translucent and reflective surfaces, and a visitor who changes the composition by moving around it.

It remains in the edit because it expands the studio’s offer beyond a flat screen and shows an approach to event environments. It is deliberately labelled as concept development.

Status: concept / development. No final client attribution, production scope, venue or delivered outcome is claimed until the underlying project record is confirmed.
