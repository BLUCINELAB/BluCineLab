The collaboration

A music project needs more than one release frame. It needs material that keeps the artist present over time.

For the 2024 Fiera di Me cycle, the scope included vertical social video, a short making-of documentary, shooting, social-edit delivery and backstage photography connected to Irene Grandi’s tour and release communication.

In 2025, the published credits for Favole name Anton Likht as director of the video loop and reel, and Sonia Giudici for concept, cinematography and editing.
