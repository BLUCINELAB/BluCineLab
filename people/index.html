02 / Project crew

The team grows because the work asks for it.

Camera, light, sound, make-up, production, technical specialists and local collaborators are added according to location, scale and production needs. They are credited for the work they actually do.

One route in. The right room around it.

Before production starts, BluCineLab defines the project lead, decision line, responsibilities and credits. Scale follows the project instead of forcing the project through a fixed agency chart.
