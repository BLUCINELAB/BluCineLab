Practice

Gesture makes the structure.

Sonia’s photographs begin with the body as a working form. Garment, architecture and ordinary places are activated through pressure, colour and an exact physical gesture.

The same attention moves into production: how an image is built, how a team is held together, and how the final sequence gains meaning.

Selected image edit

Fashion without the gloss shield.

The edit keeps the photographs that feel authored rather than merely well-produced: active bodies, uneasy crops, hard colour and surfaces that resist easy polish.

AMARSI · Artist practice

Nothing is truly finished.

Through cyanotype, discarded translucent matter becomes an unstable presence: object, memory, trace. The work connects material transformation with the act of looking after what would otherwise disappear.

Selected experience

Image making inside commercial systems and outside them.

Sonia’s professional photography experience includes work within Piquadro Group brands and an earlier e-commerce photography role at YOOX Net-A-Porter. These are personal professional experiences, not BluCineLab client credits.

Her wider practice includes direction and editing for Il viaggio, cultural-production work for Bologna Furiosa, and photography appearing on PhotoVogue and in MALVIE Magazine.
